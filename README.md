# Polarix
Polarix: Solutions That Refresh You. A web-based system that automates sales recording, inventory management, restocking requests, and cash register closing for an ice pop shop, with role-based access control.
# Project Description
Polarix is a system designed to facilitate the administration and management of an ice pop shop, allowing business information to be organized efficiently. The system aims to solve problems related to product management, inventory, sales, and suppliers, reducing errors and simplifying processes that are normally carried out manually.
## Main Features

- User authentication and role-based access control
- Product management
- Inventory tracking and stock management
- Sales registration and processing
- Automatic total and change calculation
- Inventory updates after sales
- Restocking request management
- Cash register closing and sales summaries
- Sales reports and best-selling product analysis
- Daily sales and customer tracking
